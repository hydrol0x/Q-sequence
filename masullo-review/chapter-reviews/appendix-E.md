## 3. Answers to the specific questions

- **Site.** CGWH_α is the category of CGWH spaces whose underlying set lies in V_α. Here α is the limit ordinal of Lemma E.3.2.3 for the set S_0 of Construction E.3.5.1 (Q, Z, Z_p, R, C, A_Q, closed subgroups, countable (co)products, closed quotients). No universes are used; the treatment is ZFC in Stacks-Project style.
- **Topology.** τ := J_1 is generated by families {T_i → T} with ⊔T_i → T a quotient map (Def E.3.5.2(2)), claimed to be the canonical topology. Covers are therefore not open covers; the open-cover topology is σ := J_2 and is essentially unused.
- **Fibre products.** They exist in CGWH_α (Prop E.3.4.1) and are k-ified; they are not topological fibre products in general.
- **Stack.** Def E.6.1.6 is the standard one (53911–53915).
- **Topological stack with atlas.** Never defined. "Stack in topological groupoids" (54014 onwards), "representable morphism", "coarse space", "classifying stack" and "substack" are all used undefined.
- **[X/G].** Defined by stackifying the action-groupoid prestack for any α-small CGWH group G. There is no local compactness, properness or freeness hypothesis, and principal bundles are never mentioned. Implicitly, torsors are trivialised only along quotient maps.
- **Flagged definition (footnote 11, 50850).** Correct. With Q = Y ⊔_X Y, the pullback of the two coprojections equals their equaliser because the fold map retracts both, so this is "equaliser of the cokernel pair".
- **Groupoids versus stacks.** Only Remark E.6.4.7 (54325–54328), with no proof. It is not well formed: a 1-categorical localisation is said to be "equivalent, as a 2-category" to a 2-category of stacks.
- **Chapter 8 stacks.**
  - D_{F,S} is metrisable and GL_1(A_{F,S})^1 is locally compact, so Def E.6.2.4(3) applies and the stack exists.
  - No proved result gives the equivalences of Theorem 8.3.0.1; the route would be Lemma E.6.4.2 or Example E.6.4.8, neither of which is proved.
  - The orbit relation on D_{F,S} is not closed. With two Archimedean places v ≠ w, the pairs (τ_v(t), τ_w(t)) lie in one orbit and tend to (e_v, e_w), which lie in different orbits, as t → 0. By Lemma E.2.1.11 the quotient D_{F,S}/G^1 is not weakly Hausdorff, so the naive coarse space is not an object of the site.
  - My own check supports Chapter 8's stratum formulas: [D_{S,v}/G^1] ≃ [R_{>0}/F_v^×] with F_v^× acting through the norm, inertia O_v^×, coarse space R_{>0}/q_v^Z; and BU_v at Archimedean v.

## 4. Ranked problems

1. **(D, global) E.6 does not deliver what Chapter 8 uses.** The appendix is unfinished and the notions Chapter 8 relies on (coarse space, classifying stack, the 2-isomorphisms) are neither defined nor proved here.
2. **(A) Example E.6.4.8 (54329–54339) is false twice.**
   - General claim: set-theoretic freeness is not enough. Take X = S^1 and G = H = Z acting by an irrational rotation; X/H is indiscrete, hence not in CGWH.
   - Arithmetic instance: G/H ≅ F_{v0}^× via x ↦ x_{v0} (surjective because S∖{v0} contains an Archimedean place), not O_{v0}^×. It acts on X/H ≅ R_{>0} through |·|_{v0}^{-1}, not trivially.
   - The stated conclusion [R_{>0}/O_{v0}^×] has coarse space R_{>0}. It contradicts (8.3.0.4)–(8.3.0.5), which are the correct formulas.
3. **(A) Lemma E.6.4.5 (54285–54292, no proof) is false with strict equality f∘g = h.** Take f: (pt, pt) → (R, R×R), the point 0 into the pair groupoid. It induces an equivalence, but h_1 = f_1∘g_1 is constant, so h is never a standard Morita equivalence. The statement holds only up to 2-isomorphism.
4. **(C) The site is not well defined as written.**
   - Cov_0 (Def E.3.5.2(1)) has no covering condition, so the empty family qualifies.
   - Construction E.3.5.4(1) requires Cov_0 ⊂ Cov_i. For i = 2 this is incompatible with coverings being open immersions (for example {[n, n+1] → R}).
   - The proof of Lemma E.3.5.7 describes covers as "families of universal effective epimorphisms", which open inclusions are not.
5. **(B) Prop E.6.3.8 (54137–54157): the proof is a non sequitur.** It passes from "trivial inertia" to "representable", which the text itself denies at 54146. Hypothesis (2) is vacuous. The statement is true for τ: X → X/G is a τ-cover whose kernel pair is G by hypothesis (1). The proof of Lemma E.6.3.7 is also garbled, though the statement is true.
6. **(B) Lemmas E.6.4.1–2, E.6.4.6, Remark E.6.4.7 and the "1-fibre product" Lemmas E.5.7.18–25.** These rest on analogy or on [Ac]. Lemma E.5.7.25 asserts a strict fibre product of stackifications, which is not equivalence-invariant; I could not determine whether it is correct.
7. **(A, peripheral) Results on "immersions" fail under the paper's definition (injective with the subspace topology, 48908).** This affects Lemmas E.1.2.27 and E.2.3.3 in the direction (2)⇒(1), and Lemmas E.2.3.6(3) and E.2.3.7(3).
   - Setup: T = Arens space S_2; A = S_2 with the middle level {x_n} removed. Compact subsets of A are finite, so k(A) is discrete, while A is not discrete in S_2.
   - k(A) → S_2 satisfies the test-map criterion but is not an immersion.
   - Base change: with X = {(0,0)} ∪ {(1/n, 1/m)} inside the compact metric Y = X ∪ {(1/n, 0)}, pulling X ⊂ Y back along the continuous bijection S_2 → Y gives k(A) → S_2.
   - Descent: pulling k(A) → S_2 back along the metrisable quotient cover of S_2 gives an immersion.
   - Repair: use Strickland's k-ified subspace. Closed and open immersions are unaffected.
8. **(A, minor) Example E.2.1.9 (50089–50092).** "Y Hausdorff, not locally compact ⇒ Y^+ weakly Hausdorff" is contradicted by the paper's own Example E.2.1.10 (Arens–Fort). Y must be a k-space. Also [0,1]^κ is compact, so it is not an example.
9. **(C) Lemma E.5.4.10.** The 2-cells of CGWH-Grpd are never specified. If they are continuous, the forgetful functor is not "faithful" in the sense of Def E.5.3.6(2): Const_X → (pt, G) has discontinuous natural transformations. Separately, "necessarily strict" at 53831 is wrong.
10. **(D) Proof slips with true statements.**
    - Lemma E.2.3.10: the relation R at 50732 omits the pairs (p(b), p(b′)) with q(b) = q(b′), so it is not an equivalence relation, and "(p′, q′) is injective" (50752) is false.
    - Lemma E.3.1.2: V_{α_κ+1} contains sets of cardinality greater than κ.
    - Lemma E.6.3.5: "Δ closed in Spaces" should be closed in the k-product.
    - Lemma E.4.2.6 conflates ×^0 and ×.
    - Formula typos at E.6.1.3, E.6.3.6(3), E.6.4.3(2), E.5.7.5.
