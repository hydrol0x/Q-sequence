## 2. Test case F = Q, χ = 1

Here the isotype is S(R)_even via f ↦ Σ_{n≥1} f(nx), with Mellin transform ζ(s)Mf(s).

**(i) Mellin image.** The claim is correct, in the precise form the draft states: image of S_0 = s(1−s)ζ̂(s)·O(C) ∩ AS(C), with ζ̂ completed (17791–17796; intro 2261).
- This is *not* "L·(entire functions Schwartz on strips)". The quotient u = Z/Λ is entire but grows like 1/Γ; with the finite L-function, Mf is not entire. The Γ-factors are handled by the intersection form plus the pair (φ,ψ) of ST II.
- My check of the converse: given G in the ideal, G/ζ is holomorphic on Re s > −2. It is Schwartz on Re s = 1+δ and, by the functional equation, on Re s = −δ. On horizontal segments at heights T_k where |ζ|^{-1} ≤ T^A (Titchmarsh 9.7) it is ≤ T^A|G|. The maximum principle on rectangles gives G/ζ ∈ AS(C_{>0}); the mirrored statement holds for G/ζ(1−s).
- ST II for S = {∞} (checked directly: f and f̂ both rapidly decaying with derivatives, so f ∈ S(R)) then produces f. This is the argument at 18018–18114.

**(ii) Closedness.** Yes. The image is the joint kernel of jet evaluations at the zeros, which are continuous on AS(C). The quotient topology agrees with the subspace topology by the open mapping theorem, since the source isotype is Fréchet.

**(iii) "Every isotypic θ-function is Φ_F(f_S⊗1)", and p_S.** Correct.
- A χ-isotypic element with χ unramified outside S is K^S-invariant, hence the image of a K^S-invariant Schwartz function, i.e. a sum of f_S ⊗ 1_{𝔞Ô^S}.
- Pic(O_{F,S}) = 1 is exactly what gives ξ ∈ F^× with ξÔ^S = 𝔞Ô^S, so each term is F^×-equivalent to some f'_S ⊗ 1.
- Without it the K^S-invariants split into several pieces indexed by ideal classes and π^S hits only one.
- p_S is then a continuous bijection from a Fréchet space onto a quotient of an LF-space, hence an isomorphism. This bypasses the broken embedding proof (A3).

**(iv) PV closed range.**
- Fine topology: true on isotypes. For S = {∞,p}, PV_p is f ↦ f − f(·/p) on S(R)_even. Its range is {g : g(0) = 0, Mg(2πik/log p) = 0 for k ≠ 0}; I checked that f = −Σ_{j≥1} g(p^j·) is Schwartz exactly under these conditions. That is closed, so PV_p is an embedding by open mapping. The mechanism is division by 1 − p^{-s} inside functions holomorphic across Re s = 0.
- The draft's proof (13690–13693) instead infers the embedding property from "injective + composition of a relatively open map with a quotient map", which does not follow.
- Hilbert norms: it fails. On Re s = a ≠ 0 the multiplier is bounded above and below, so PV is invertible and the cokernel vanishes. On Re s = 0 with weight (1+log²|g|)^s (Mellin side H^s(R); this is how I read line 26002) the multiplier has simple zeros on the line and is not bounded below: g_ε = ε^{-1/2}φ((t−t_0)/ε) gives ‖mg_ε‖/‖g_ε‖ ≲ ε. So the range is not closed for any s.
- This contradicts 29217–29224 and Prop 11.2.0.1(2) (29233–29275), which assert closed range after completion, if I read the norm correctly. That is outside my range; the Chapter 11 reviewer should confirm.

**(v) Analytic inputs.**
- Polynomial growth of L in strips (convexity; 17883–17886): makes multiplication by L^{Σ−S} continuous on AS. Sound.
- Horizontal lines with |1/L| ≤ T^A (Cor B.6.1.8; 17922–17929, 18031, 18085–18090): sound in the converse half. Not sufficient as cited for closedness of T_{S,χ} (B4).
- Non-vanishing on Re s = 1: used once in range (18004, completed L non-vanishing on Re s ≤ 0), where it is dispensable since Z/L is entire by local theory. It is essential in Ch. 8 via Thm 7.5.0.1 (Euler-factor zeros on Re s = 0 must avoid zeros of L) and in the density claim at 29239.

## 5. Ranked problems

**(A) False as stated**
1. **Thm 8.1.0.1 (22807–22836), principal χ.** The functional f ↦ f(0) on A_{F,S} descends to the target, kills the PV-range and kills S_p(A_{F,S}), yet is nonzero. So the map is not surjective. For F=Q, S={∞,p} it is not injective either: take f ≡ 1 near 0, g = f − f(·/p).
   - The proof silently replaces S by S_0 (22846).
   - Identity (8.1.0.9) (22890) is false: Λ(s,χ)·1 lies in the left side, but 1 ∉ AS(C).
   - "Let ϕ ∈ AS(C)" (22897) is therefore unjustified.
2. **Lemma 5.3.1.1 (13506–13540).** For a character ω ≠ 1 of O_v^×, f = ω·1_{O_v^×} has f(0) = 0 but is not in the range of P_v: on the ω-isotype ≅ C[z^{±1}], P_v = 1−z, of codimension 1. P_v also depends on π_v off the invariants. The "WLOG invariant" step (13524–13526) is the error. Harmless for PV_v.
3. **Step 12959–12961: Π_S(ker Φ_F) ⊂ ker Φ_F.** False for every Π_S of Lemma 2.1.4.3. For f = (1−ρ(ξ))(h⊗1_K) with ξ a non-S-unit, Z(Π_S f) = Z_S(h)L^S(c)[1 − φ̃(1_{ξK})c_S(ξ)] ≢ 0. Reused at 13129. This invalidates the written proofs of 5.2.1.1(2)(3) and 5.2.3.1(1)(3). The statements are true by K-isotypic decomposition.
4. **(Outside range) closed range of PV in W^{s,2}_0** (29217–29235); see §2(iv).

**(B) Gaps**
1. **Completeness in 5.2.1.1(1) (12925–12928).** Schaefer II.6.6 needs a strict inductive limit; a Hausdorff quotient of a strict LF-space need not be strict or complete. Missing: the decomposition into Fréchet K-isotypes, which does prove it. Completeness feeds every Pettis-integral projector.
2. **Lemma 5.3.1.5 proof** (13679–13693).
3. **Thm 5.4.1.1 proof.** At 15636–15649, "completion Ĥ surjects onto H" has the map backwards; at 15689–15726 the spanning property is asserted, not shown.
4. **Thm 6.3.2.1, closed range of T_{S,χ} (17902–17936).** φ̃/L^{Σ−S} lives only on C_{>0}, so the strip C_{[−ε,1+ε]} argument does not apply. Def B.3.1.1(3) needs 1/L polynomially bounded on a vertical line inside the critical strip, which is not known. Fix: choose σ_0 by averaging Σ log|σ_0 − β|, or deduce closedness from the explicit range.

2. **Thm 8.3.0.1 (23164–23173, 23215).** A Schwartz function "supported in" the closed nowhere-dense set H_{S,S_p} is zero. What is meant is the support of the distributions.
3. **Thm 6.3.2.1(2) (17822).** The middle term "ζ̂·O(C) ∩ AS(C)" contains functions with poles.
